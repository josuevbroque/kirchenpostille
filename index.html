Welcome

Luther’s Church Postil

A day-by-day reader for Martin Luther’s sermons on the Sunday and festival Gospel and Epistle readings of the church year.

What it is based on

The Church Postil (Kirchenpostille) is Luther’s collection of model sermons, written to be read aloud in the home and the congregation. Each sermon expounds the appointed Gospel or Epistle for a day of the church year.

The text is taken from Martin Luther’s Church Postil — The Complete Lenker Edition in One Volume, shown unaltered.

Which calendar

This site follows the old, historic one-year calendar and lectionary that the Postil is built on: Advent, Christmas, Epiphany, Septuagesima, Lent, Easter, Pentecost, and the long season of Sundays after Trinity. It does not use the modern three-year cycle (Years A, B and C), so the readings for a given Sunday are the same every year.

How it works

Today’s sermon opens automatically. The site works out the church year from the date.

No sermon on this date? You are shown the sermon for the current church week, and a note tells you so.

Several sermons on one day? Choose between them under “Sermons today” in the menu.

Getting around

Turn pages with the arrow buttons, the ← → keys, a swipe, or a tap at the edge of the page.

The menu (top right) lets you jump to a section, step to the previous or next postil, change the text size, open the calendar, or browse all days.

The accent colour follows the liturgical season.
